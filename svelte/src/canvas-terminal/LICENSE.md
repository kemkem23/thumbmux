# Centerline glyph license

The geometric centerline glyphs generated by `glyphs.ts` were created for
Thumbmux on 2026-09-24 and are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

They cover ASCII U+0020–U+007E and Box Drawing U+2500–U+257F. Thai and the
declared CJK sample deliberately use the labelled outline fallback; they are
not claimed as single-line coverage.
